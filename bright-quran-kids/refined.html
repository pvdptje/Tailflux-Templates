Quran Lessons for Kids

Our structured curriculum helps children learn Quranic Arabic, proper pronunciation (Tajweed), and memorization of short Surahs. Lessons are designed to be engaging and age-appropriate with interactive exercises.

  • Learn Arabic alphabet with sounds & writing
  • Short Surahs memorization (Al-Fatiha, Al-Ikhlas, etc.)
  • Basic Tajweed rules with voice recognition

Islamic Educational Games

Learning through play! Our collection of games helps reinforce Quranic knowledge, Islamic values, and Arabic language skills in a fun, interactive way that children love.

  • Arabic letter matching & memory games
  • Islamic trivia, quizzes, and puzzles
  • Interactive Quranic verse puzzles

Prophets Stories & Islamic History

Engaging animated stories of the Prophets, companions of the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH), and important events in Islamic history. Designed to teach moral values and Islamic principles through captivating narratives.

  • Animated story of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH)

  • Stories of the companions (Sahaba) with lessons
