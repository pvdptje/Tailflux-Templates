Building Peace Since 2010

Empowering Youth to Build Peace in Their Communities

YouthPeace Alliance is a global NGO dedicated to fostering peace, dialogue, and understanding among young people. We provide tools, training, and platforms for youth to become peacebuilders in their communities.

YouthPeace Alliance

Connecting 15,000+ young peacebuilders worldwide

Our Mission & Vision

We believe that young people are not just the leaders of tomorrow, but the peacebuilders of today. Our mission is to equip them with the skills and opportunities to create lasting change.

Peace Education

We develop and deliver comprehensive peace education curricula for schools and youth organizations, focusing on conflict resolution, empathy, and intercultural dialogue.

Youth Dialogues

We facilitate safe spaces for young people from diverse backgrounds to engage in meaningful dialogue, share experiences, and build mutual understanding.

Community Projects

We support youth-led peacebuilding initiatives with funding, mentorship, and resources to implement tangible projects in their local communities.

Our Programs

We offer a range of programs designed to engage youth at different levels and from various backgrounds in peacebuilding activities.

Peace Leadership Academy

A year-long intensive program for emerging youth leaders aged 18-25, combining online learning with in-person workshops and mentorship.

Virtual & In-person Ages 18-25 Scholarships Available

School Peace Clubs

Establishing and supporting peace clubs in secondary schools to promote peer mediation, anti-bullying initiatives, and inclusive school environments.

Ages 13-18 Global Network Teacher Training

Featured Story: Maria's Journey

Maria joined our Peace Leadership Academy in Colombia after witnessing community violence. Through our program, she developed skills in mediation and dialogue facilitation.

Today, Maria leads a youth peace council in her neighborhood that has successfully mediated 15 community conflicts and established a community garden that brings together former rival groups.
